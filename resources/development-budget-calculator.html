What affects mobile app development cost?

  • App type: A simple utility app generally needs a different scope from an eCommerce or custom business platform.
  • Platform: Supporting Android and iOS can require additional implementation and testing.
  • Features: Payments, dashboards, chat, maps and integrations add development work.
  • Backend: Accounts, APIs, data synchronization and business logic can require server-side development.
  • Design and quality: Custom interfaces, testing and deployment affect project scope.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the final price for my app?

No. It is an indicative planning range based on the selections and assumptions shown. A final quotation requires a review of your detailed requirements.

Does the estimate include hosting and third-party fees?

No. Hosting, payment gateway charges, app-store fees, paid APIs and other third-party costs are excluded unless separately agreed.

Is maintenance included in the development price?

No. The calculator displays optional monthly maintenance separately from the one-time development estimate.

How can I get an accurate quotation?

Prepare your app requirements, including user roles, screens, workflows, integrations and launch expectations, then contact eHKAR for a project assessment.
