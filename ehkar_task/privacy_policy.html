Privacy Policy for eHKAR Task

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 · Built by eHKAR Software

eHKAR Software ("we", "our", or "us") built the eHKAR Task mobile application as a fast, offline-first productivity tool. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app.

1. 100% Offline Local Storage

eHKAR Task is designed with privacy first. All tasks, notes, categories, priorities, due dates, reminder schedules, and app preferences you create are stored exclusively on your device using local storage. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your personal data on external servers.

2. Permissions We Request

To deliver core task reminder features, eHKAR Task may request the following Android permissions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to display local task reminder notifications when a scheduled reminder triggers.
  • Exact Alarms & Foreground Service (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Used to trigger your scheduled task reminders accurately on time, even when the app is closed or your device is locked.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

eHKAR Task does not require account registration and does not use third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, or external analytics services that collect personal user data.

4. Data Deletion & Control

Because all data remains locally on your phone, you have complete control over it. You can edit or delete individual tasks at any time inside the app, or remove all app data permanently by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling eHKAR Task from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

eHKAR Task does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as new features are introduced. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
